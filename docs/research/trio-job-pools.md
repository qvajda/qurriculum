# Candidate trios ranked by job pool size

Retrieved 2026-10-04. Refs #38, epic #4. Scope: size only. Not Etsy demand or price (#8),
not CV content differences, not build order.

Revision: the first cut used ISCO-08 major groups (1-digit). The owner rejected it on
PR #40 as too coarse (`OC2` Professionals lumps engineers, doctors, teachers and IT
together). This version ranks ISCO-08 sub-major groups (2-digit) instead.

## Method

- **Trio** = `COUNTRY:LANGUAGE:FIELD`, e.g. `BE:FR:OC25`.
- **Job pool** = employed persons aged 15-74, thousands, annual 2024.
- **Field scheme**: ISCO-08 sub-major groups (2-digit, `OC11`-`OC96`, `OC01`-`OC03`)
  for every country. No other occupation scheme is used anywhere in this table.
- **Primary source for every figure**: Eurostat LFS, dataset `lfsa_egai2d`
  (employed persons by detailed occupation, ISCO-08 two digit level). Language splits
  use Eurostat `lfst_r_lfe2emp` (employed persons by NUTS 2 region). Both 2024 annual.
  No national statistics office was needed: Eurostat covers every country asked for.
- **Countries**: BE, FR, DE, AT, CH, NL, PL, ES, IT, SE, DK, NO, FI (#12's set).
- **Languages**: one working language per country except BE (FR, NL) and CH
  (DE, FR, IT). Minority languages (e.g. SV in FI, CA in ES) are not split out:
  no sourced share was gathered, and D-5 forbids an unsourced one.
- **Language split rule**: trio pool = national 2-digit count x the language
  region's share of national employment (NUTS 2, source above). Assumes the
  occupation mix inside a region equals the national mix; untested, because
  these Eurostat tables give occupation only nationally.
  - BE: NL = BE2 Flanders (3090.3 of 5056.3 thousand employed), FR = BE3 Wallonia
    (1453.4 of 5056.3). BE1 Brussels (512.6) is bilingual and counted in neither,
    so BE:FR is understated.
  - CH: DE = CH03+CH04+CH05+CH06 (2762.7 of 4829.5), FR = CH01 Lac Leman (843.6),
    IT = CH07 Ticino (164.7). CH02 Espace Mittelland (1058.5) mixes DE and FR and is
    counted in neither, so CH:DE and CH:FR are understated.
- **Flags**: the `flag` column carries Eurostat's status flag verbatim: `u` low
  reliability, `d` definition differs, `bd` break in series and definition differs.
  32 rows carry `u`, none of them in the launch set.
- **Suppressed cells**: Eurostat publishes no 2024 figure for 35 country x group
  cells (mostly `OC63` subsistence workers, `OC95` street vendors, and armed-forces
  ranks): BE OC62 OC63 OC95; DK OC63 OC95; DE OC63 OC95 OC01 OC02; ES OC63;
  FR OC63 OC95; IT OC63; NL OC63 OC95; AT OC63 OC95 OC01 OC03; PL OC63 OC95;
  FI OC63 OC03; SE OC63 OC95 OC02; NO OC63 OC73 OC92 OC95 OC01 OC02 OC03; CH OC63 OC95.
  Those trios have no row. All are too small to reach the top 30.
- **Ranking**: descending pool, 646 trios. Rank 30 is DE:DE:OC91 at 1163.5 thousand;
  rank 31 is FR:FR:OC91 at 1132.2 thousand.
- **Country mix**: the top 30 is DE 15, FR 6, IT 5, ES 3, PL 1. NL's largest trio,
  NL:NL:OC24 (913.7), ranks 57. At 1-digit NL:NL:OC2 made the top 30; at 2-digit no
  single NL group is large enough.
