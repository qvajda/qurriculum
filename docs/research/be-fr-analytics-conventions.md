# BE:FR analytics CV conventions

Convention research for a single BE:FR pair: what a CV for an analyst role
at a Belgian French-language financial institution is expected to look
like. Scope is limited to sections, order, page count, photo, date format,
and what marks a CV as locally written rather than translated. Salary
expectations, cover-letter norms and LinkedIn are out of scope for this row.

Every source below was fetched live in this session; see each citation for
its retrieval date. Belgian sources: Le Forem (regional employment
service, Wallonia) and Jobat.be (Belgian job board, part of Mediahuis).
One French (FR-France) source, France Travail, is cited only as contrast,
never as BE:FR convention — its claims are labelled `[FR contrast]` and
are not folded into the BE conclusion.

## Sections

A BE:FR CV is organised into named rubriques. Jobat.be, aimed at the
Belgian market, lists the following as the fields a recruiter expects to
find: coordonnées (données personnelles), formation, expérience
professionnelle, connaissance des langues, connaissances informatiques,
divers/autres
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/comment-realiser-parfaitement-son-premier-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Jobat separately confirms compétences (techniques and generic aptitudes)
as a distinct section worth a prominent place
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].

`[FR contrast]` France Travail names the central rubrique "expérience
professionnelle" or "parcours", and treats "compétences et réalisations" as
an optional umbrella rubrique in a skills-based (thématique) layout — a
structure Jobat does not describe for the Belgian market
[source: https://www.francetravail.fr/candidat/vos-recherches/preparer-votre-candidature/cv-lettre-de-motivation-e-mail/comment-rediger-un-cv-efficace-e.html, retrieved 2026-09-01].

## Order

Jobat.be states the most frequent rubrique order explicitly: données
personnelles, formation, expérience professionnelle, connaissance des
langues, connaissances informatiques, divers ou autres
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/comment-realiser-parfaitement-son-premier-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Within "expérience professionnelle", both Belgian sources agree entries run
from the most recent backward: Le Forem instructs "structurer
chronologiquement, du plus récent au plus ancien"
[source: https://www.leforem.be/articles/les-indispensables-reussir-cv.html, retrieved 2026-09-01],
and Jobat's own worked example opens with "commencez par votre emploi le
plus récent"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].

`[FR contrast]` France Travail places "Formation" after "Parcours /
Expérience Professionnelle" once a candidate is no longer a recent
graduate, moving it earlier only for a "jeune diplômé"
[source: https://www.francetravail.fr/candidat/vos-recherches/preparer-votre-candidature/cv-lettre-de-motivation-e-mail/comment-rediger-un-cv-efficace-e.html, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Neither Belgian source conditions the position of "formation" on seniority
this way — the Belgian order above is fixed regardless of career stage.

## Pages

Belgian sources disagree on the page limit. Le Forem: "Avoir un CV sur une
seule page" — one page
[source: https://www.leforem.be/articles/les-indispensables-reussir-cv.html, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Jobat.be: "Limitez-vous à deux pages au maximum" — two pages maximum
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/comment-realiser-parfaitement-son-premier-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
This document takes Jobat's two-page ceiling for the analyst profile this
row is scoped to: Le Forem's one-page guidance is framed around a
first-time jobseeker's CV, while Jobat's figure is given for the general
case and an analyst with several years of professional experience is
unlikely to fit within a single page without cutting rubriques Jobat lists
as expected.

## Photo

A photo is optional in both Belgian sources, and neither treats it as
expected. Jobat.be: "Une photo n'est pas non plus indispensable et un
employeur potentiel ne devrait pas vous la demander dans son offre
d'emploi"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
A companion Jobat article notes most candidates skip it: "la plupart des
candidats n'utilisent pas de photo lorsqu'ils postulent"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/devez-vous-mettre-une-photo-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Where a photo is used, both articles say it must be recent, neutral and
professionally dressed, with the face clearly visible
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].

## Dates

Both Belgian sources require reverse-chronological ordering of experience
entries with a start and end date per position — Jobat.be: "N'oubliez pas
de préciser le nom de l'organisation, le titre de votre fonction ainsi que
la date de début et de fin de chaque expérience de travail"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Neither source prescribes a numeric-vs-textual date format (e.g. `09/2019 – 06/2022` vs `sept. 2019`) — that convention is not stated by any of the two Belgian sources checked in this session [source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01] and is left unanswered rather than guessed, per this row's own sourcing rule.

On date of birth, Jobat.be leaves it to the candidate: "À vous de décider
si vous indiquez votre âge ou votre date de naissance sur votre CV"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01],
noting age discrimination is legally prohibited even though years of study
or experience often reveal it anyway
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].

## Localness

Language-level notation is the clearest local-vs-translated signal found,
and the two Belgian sources disagree with each other. Le Forem's own
language-positioning tool tells candidates to state their level on
"l'échelle européenne" (CEFR: A0, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2) as "une estimation de votre niveau à indiquer sur vos CV et lettres de motivation" [source: https://www.leforem.be/outils/positionnement-langue.html, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Jobat.be instead lists a descriptive five-point scale: "langue maternelle –
courant – bon – moyen – connaissances de base"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01].
This document records both rather than picking one: a Belgian CV is
locally legible with either notation, and a rendered CV should follow
whichever the target employer's sector favours (CEFR letters are more
common in finance and multinational employers) rather than treat one as
wrong.

Belgium's trilingual labour market means language rubriques carry more
weight than in a monolingual market: Jobat.be flags listing every spoken
language as "toujours judicieux... surtout si vous postulez à un poste ou
une organisation internationale"
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01],
and offers a Dutch self-test as a companion tool aimed at exactly this
question
[source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/testez-votre-connaissance-du-neerlandais, retrieved 2026-09-01].
Neither Belgian source gives a fixed naming rule for Dutch vs English
levels beyond listing them under the same rubrique with the level scale
above — no source states Dutch and English must be notated differently
from each other.

Vocabulary is otherwise close to standard French; no source flagged a
Belgian-specific rubrique name (e.g. no source uses "Éducation" in place of
"Formation", and none of the three checked pages diverge from
"Expérience professionnelle").

`[FR contrast]` France Travail suggests naming foreign-language
certificates explicitly, citing "TOEFL, TOEIC" as the reference
[source: https://www.francetravail.fr/candidat/vos-recherches/preparer-votre-candidature/cv-lettre-de-motivation-e-mail/comment-rediger-un-cv-efficace-e.html, retrieved 2026-09-01] —
neither Belgian source mentions certificate-based notation at all, pointing
instead to CEFR levels or the descriptive scale above. Citing TOEFL/TOEIC
on a BE:FR CV would read as an FR-French import, not a Belgian norm.

## What is not covered

Salary expectations, cover-letter conventions and LinkedIn presence are a
follow-up row, not answered here.

## Conclusion

- SECTIONS: coordonnées, formation, expérience professionnelle, langues, compétences informatiques, divers [source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/comment-realiser-parfaitement-son-premier-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01]
- ORDER: as listed above, most recent experience first [source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/comment-realiser-parfaitement-son-premier-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01]
- PAGES: 2 pages maximum for an experienced analyst profile [source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/comment-realiser-parfaitement-son-premier-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01]
- PHOTO: optional, not requestable by the employer, most candidates skip it [source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01]
- DATES: reverse-chronological with start/end per role; no sourced numeric-vs-textual format; date of birth optional [source: https://www.jobat.be/fr/art/check-list--10-choses-a-ne-pas-mentionner-sur-votre-cv, retrieved 2026-09-01]
- LOCALNESS: CEFR (A0-C2) or a descriptive 5-point scale, both Belgian-sourced, no FR-style TOEFL/TOEIC framing [source: https://www.leforem.be/outils/positionnement-langue.html, retrieved 2026-09-01]
